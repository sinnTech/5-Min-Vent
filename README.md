# The 5-Minute Vent

### *Five minutes. No filter. No forever.*

---

## 🧘 What is this?

**The 5-Minute Vent** is a judgement-free digital pressure valve.

Choose your time: **3, 5, or 10 minutes**. Then type anything — rage, sadness, nonsense, things you'd never say out loud.

The bot doesn't fix, therapize, or quote inspirational Instagram posts. It just listens.

> *"Go on."*
> *"I'm listening."*
> *"Keep going."*

When the timer ends, you choose:
- 🔥 **Burn it** — permanent delete, gone forever
- 🌙 **Let it fade** — encrypted for 24 hours, then auto-delete

No history. No "your data helps us improve." Just catharsis.

---

## ✨ Features

- ⏱️ **3, 5, or 10 minute timers** — choose your vent length
- 💬 **Simple listening bot** — no advice, no judgment
- 🔥 **Instant burn** — permanent deletion with visual fire animation
- 🌙 **24-hour fade** — words disappear tomorrow
- 📱 **Mobile friendly** — works on any device
- 🕊️ **Zero tracking** — no accounts, no cookies, no analytics

## 🧠 The Philosophy

$$\text{Catharsis} = \frac{\text{Expression}}{\text{Consequence}}$$

When consequences approach zero, honesty approaches infinity.

This app doesn't want to *fix* you. It wants to *hold space* for you — then forget everything you said.
